## What it shows

- **Ryan and the no-skills setup passed every safety check in all three runs; Matt's setup failed one.** One failure in three runs is an event, not a rate: it does not show that Matt's skills are unsafe, or that Ryan's are safe in production.
- **Ryan's own flow used about a third of Matt's fresh tokens:** 64k against 185k, with the same result. Most of Matt's came from subagents.
- **On small tasks Ryan uses 5k to 11k more fresh tokens.** It reads its rules and follows `autonomy.md`, which the bench setup set to ask before every commit, so it often ends its turn with a question. The simulated user answers, and that extra turn re-reads the whole conversation (see total tokens). On the plain `tags` prompt, "plan it first" also started `ryan-plan`, as its description says it should.
- **`camel-case` has no right rename.** The only snake_case name in `src/` is `created_at`, which is part of the public contract. Codex renamed it internally and kept the contract; Claude mostly left it alone. Both pass.

## Found and fixed
